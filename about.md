I'm a frontend developer based in Concord, North Carolina, with over a decade of experience building and maintaining websites. My wheelhouse is WordPress — I've worked on both the agency and client sides of things, building custom ACF and Gutenberg blocks, wrangling legacy code into shape, and making sure the things I ship actually work the way they're supposed to across every device and browser. I take a lot of pride in that last part.

Most recently I've been doing frontend work at WordPress agencies, where I've gotten to flex both my development chops and my inner QA nerd. There's something genuinely satisfying about combing through a site and catching the things that nobody else noticed yet.

When I'm not at the keyboard I'm usually playing guitar, tinkering with electronics, or recording a podcast with some friends.
